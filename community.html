Four house rules

  1. Be kind. To people and their pets. No harassment, slurs or pile-ons.
  2. Tag spoilers for Mythics and hidden places, outside #glimmerwild.
  3. Keep it safe for everyone. No NSFW, no scams, no selling things.
  4. Never share an API key or anything from your %LOCALAPPDATA% folder. Nobody from Glitchlings will ever ask for one.

Free, and staying free

Every Glitchling is free and always will be. Nothing in the game is for sale, and a donation never changes what hatches, how a battle goes or how your pet feels about you. It just helps the project keep going.

Where it goes

  • The friends' server. The always-on server that carries visits, hangouts and live battles.
  • A code-signing certificate, so Windows stops warning you when you open the app.
  • API credits for players who can't afford a key, so their pets can talk too.
  • Art and animation for new species, places and moves.
  • A proper home on the web, with its own domain.

Supporters get

  • A Supporter role and name colour on the Discord, and a channel to see what's coming next.
  • That's it, on purpose. Every pet, place and move is the same for everyone.

Free ways to help

  • Invite a friend. Right-click your pet and choose invite a friend.
  • Report a bug in the Discord. It's a beta; every report makes it better.
  • Share a screenshot of your pet somewhere people will see it.
